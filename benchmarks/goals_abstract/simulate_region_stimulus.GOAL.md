# Goal: Stimulating a Specific Brain Region

## Scientific Question
I want to apply an external stimulus to specific brain regions and observe how the evoked response propagates through the network. How do I do this?

## Expected Output
A Jupyter notebook that:
- Sets up a brain network model with directed stimulation to chosen regions
- Shows the time course of the evoked response in both stimulated and downstream regions
- Demonstrates how activity spreads along structural connections
- Quantifies propagation delays and response amplitudes
