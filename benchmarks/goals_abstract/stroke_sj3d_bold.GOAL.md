# Goal: Stroke Recovery Modeling

## Scientific Question
After a stroke, the brain reorganizes its functional connections. How can I model the effect of a structural lesion on brain dynamics and assess recovery potential?

## Expected Output
A Jupyter notebook that:
- Creates a lesioned brain network by removing connections to/from a motor region
- Explores how global coupling and conduction velocity affect post-lesion dynamics
- Runs a long simulation to generate realistic BOLD signals
- Validates the simulated BOLD against known properties (amplitude, frequency, FC structure)
- Discusses what the structure-function relationship tells us about recovery
