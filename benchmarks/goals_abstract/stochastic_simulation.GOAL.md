# Goal: Stochastic Brain Simulations

## Scientific Question
The brain is inherently noisy. How do I add stochastic (random) fluctuations to a neural simulation, and how does noise affect the reliability of my results?

## Expected Output
A Jupyter notebook that:
- Runs a brain simulation with and without stochastic noise
- Compares deterministic vs stochastic dynamics
- Shows how noise affects functional connectivity estimates
- Discusses the tradeoffs between realism and reproducibility
- Demonstrates that results are robust across multiple noise realizations
