# Goal: Simulating BOLD fMRI Signals

## Scientific Question
How do I simulate realistic BOLD (blood-oxygen-level-dependent) fMRI signals from a neural mass model, and what can I learn from the hemodynamic response?

## Expected Output
A Jupyter notebook that:
- Runs a neural simulation that produces BOLD-like output
- Explores how the hemodynamic response shapes the observed signal
- Compares different hemodynamic response kernels
- Produces functional connectivity matrices from simulated BOLD
- Validates that the BOLD signals have realistic properties (amplitude, frequency, structure-function correlation)
